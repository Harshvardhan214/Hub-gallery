Reel 01 — About

Light is the only
character I direct.

I started as a gaffer's assistant on a fishing dock in Bergen, learning what a single 2K tungsten head can do to a grey afternoon. That's still the job: deciding what the audience is allowed to see, and when.

Today I split my time between narrative features and commercial work that still wants to look like cinema — not content. Every project starts on film stock in my head, even when it finishes on a sensor.
